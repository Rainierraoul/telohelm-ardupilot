# What each paper 3 task tells the learner

Generated by `protocol/task_texts.py` from `ardupilot/pack.py`. The shared text below is part of every task's goal text. The goal values each scenario fills in (targets, radii, limits) come from the scenario generator and are not listed here.

## Shared goal text

Positions and velocities are the autopilot's own estimate, in metres north and east of home; alt_m is metres above home. Each step is one simulated second, and the vehicle cannot stop instantly. Never go further than ctx.goal.fence_m from home. ctx.threats lists the threat zones active now, each with north_m, east_m and radius_m; entering one at any step fails the episode. gps_fix_type and gps_satellites come from the GPS receiver (MAVLink GPS_FIX_TYPE), ekf_flags is the autopilot's position-estimate status (MAVLink EKF_STATUS_FLAGS), battery_v is the battery voltage. If the autopilot leaves guided mode on its own, the episode fails.
