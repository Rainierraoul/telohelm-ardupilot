Kinds of change, written after reading 11 pairs (reach b0 b3 b5 b23; reach3d b0 b3 b17; land b4 b6 b10 b14)
and before classifying the rest. Written 22 Nov.

Keep-out distance = the buffer m in R = radius + m that the code steers around: the inflated circle of a
tangent method, the side offset of a detour waypoint, the onset distance of a repulsion field, or the
clearance constraint of a simulated search. Recorded per source, with the trigger buffer if different.

K1 smaller keep-out: the repair's keep-out distance (for the close layout, target 3-4 m beyond the far
   edge) is smaller than c0's.
K2 keep-out tied to the target: the repair computes the buffer from the target's distance to the zone
   (e.g. min(cap, target-to-edge minus k) with a floor), or drops/shrinks the buffer or push-out when the
   target lies inside it. c0 did not.
K3 avoidance method changed: primary method differs between c0 and repair, methods being
   F field (adds away/tangential vectors weighted by distance to the goal velocity),
   W waypoint (aims at a point placed beside the zone),
   T tangent heading (heads along the tangent to the inflated circle, asin(R/D)),
   S sampled forward simulation (tries candidate commands, simulates ahead, picks by cost),
   O other.
K4 delay or lag handled differently: repair adds or changes a position prediction ahead of the
   observation (n + k*vn), or a modelled response lag (time constant, alpha, a 0.3 s offset) used in
   steering or simulation.
K5 speed near the zone: repair adds or changes a speed limit that applies only while avoiding or within
   some distance of the zone, including a limit on the velocity component toward the zone.
K6 side choice changed: the quantity used to pick left or right differs (e.g. current velocity vs target
   bearing), or a remembered/sticky side is added. A rewrite of the same test does not count.
K7 trigger changed: the test deciding whether a zone needs avoiding looks at something different
   (distance to centre vs path segment to target; only zones ahead; lookahead length). A change only of
   the buffer number does not count.
K8 horizontal approach near the target changed: gain or limit of speed with distance to target,
   damping by current velocity near the target, or an added slow-down zone.
K9 push-out rule changed: the rule applied when inside the buffer (radial push distance or strength)
   added, removed or changed in radius or strength.
K10 descent rule changed (land, and altitude for reach3d only if it touches the zone): when or how fast
   the vehicle descends, e.g. only after horizontal speed is low, or no descent while avoiding.

Amendments made while classifying the first 14 pairs (before the remaining 70):
- Method classes added: P sidestep (while the path is blocked, replaces or blends the heading with a
  perpendicular to the target line, plus optional push), H heading search (tries headings in steps and
  takes the first whose straight ray is clear of an inflated circle). T covers both the tangent line
  from the vehicle (asin(R/D)) and following the circle perpendicular to the line of sight.
- When the repair switches to S, side (K6) and trigger (K7) come out of the search and are not counted;
  removal of c0's push-out with the switch is covered by K3, not K9. K8 for an S repair counts only for an
  explicit near-target rule (speed cap with distance, damping) that differs from c0.
- K9 counts when the repair adds an explicit inside-buffer rule or changes its radius or strength.
- Method class V added at pair 27 (reach b30): filter (computes the goal command, then removes or limits
  the command's component toward the zone, sometimes adding a tangential part).
